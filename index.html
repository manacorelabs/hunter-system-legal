Hunter System Privacy Policy

Effective: November 11, 2026

Hunter System ("the app", "we") is made by Mana Core Labs. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how to delete it. Contact: manacorelabs@gmail.com

What we collect

Account

  • Your email address and sign-in details (passwords are stored hashed by our sign-in provider; we never see them).
  • Your hunter name, username, display name, bio, aura colour, title, friend code and settings.
  • A profile picture, only if you upload one.

Fitness and health

  • With your permission, the app reads workouts, steps, heart rate, resting heart rate and sleep from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS).
  • Workouts: for each workout we store its type, start and end time, minutes, distance, energy burned, steps, the app that recorded it, and your average and peak heart rate during it. This turns your training into XP, stats, quests and leaderboard minutes. A workout whose average heart rate is well above your resting heart rate counts as Intense and does more damage in breaches.
  • Resting heart rate: read on your phone to show your trend on the Status screen. Your 7-day average is sent with each training sync to decide which workouts are Intense; it is not stored.
  • Sleep: last night's sleep is read on your phone and shown on the Status screen. If you claim the daily Rested reward, we store that night's date and how many minutes you slept.
  • Steps today are read on your phone for step quests and the Status screen.
  • Your Awakening answers: your fitness goal, the activity readiness (safety) questions, your optional fitness test results and your optional wellbeing questionnaire scores. They set your starting rank and whether the app suggests lower-intensity training.
  • Your progress in the app: level, XP, stats, rank, streaks, quests, gates, dungeons, shadows, items and currency.
  • We do not sell health data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with anyone except the service providers listed below that store it for us.

Location

  • Precise location, only while you use gate features (finding gates near you, checking in at a gate, dungeon floors). Searching for nearby gates uses your location to sort results and does not store it. When you check in at a gate or a dungeon floor, we store the coordinates of that check-in with the visit, to confirm you were there.
  • The app does not track your location in the background.

Social

  • Friends, friend requests, duels and guild (squad) membership.
  • Hunters you block and reports you send. Reports include the reason, optional details you write, and where in the app you sent them from. The reported hunter is not told who reported them.

Notifications

  • If you allow notifications, a push token from Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send you reminders and alerts. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone settings.

Usage and errors

  • Which screens you open and actions you take in the app (for example "quest completed"), with the app version, platform (Android or iOS) and a random session id. Errors and crashes with their technical details. We use these to fix bugs and improve the app. This is not used for advertising.

Payments

  • If you subscribe, payment is handled by Stripe (and by Google Play or the App Store where those are used). We never see or store your card number. We store your subscription status, plan, billing period and Stripe customer and subscription ids.

What we do not collect

  • We do not show ads and do not use advertising ids.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • We do not read your contacts, messages, photos (other than a picture you choose to upload), or microphone.

Who we share it with

Only these service providers, who process data on our behalf to run the app:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, file storage and server functions (stores all of the data above).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers push notifications.
  • Stripe: processes subscription payments.
  • Google Fonts: the app may download its fonts from Google, which sees your device's IP address.

Other hunters see only what the game shows: your hunter name, level, title, aura, profile picture, weekly training minutes and leaderboard scores. They never see your email, health details, location or Awakening answers.

We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account data is kept until you delete your account.
  • Usage events and error logs are deleted after 12 months.
  • Closed reports are deleted after 2 years.
  • When you delete your account, everything tied to it is deleted right away (see below). Backups held by our database provider roll off within their normal backup window.

Delete your account

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Profile → Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your profile, progress, health, workout and sleep records the app stored, Awakening answers, location check-ins, shadows, items, currency, friends, duels, guild memberships, profile picture, push token and usage logs. An active subscription is cancelled first. Guilds you created stay for their other members. Data in your phone's health app is not touched.

If you can no longer open the app, email manacorelabs@gmail.com from your account's email address and ask us to delete your account. We will do it within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • You can revoke health, location and notification permissions in your phone settings at any time. The app keeps working with fewer features.
  • You can edit your profile in the app and delete your account as above.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to its use. Email manacorelabs@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Hunter System is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access rules so each hunter can only read their own private data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect yours.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
